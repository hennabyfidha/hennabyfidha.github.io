100% Pure & Chemical-Free

The Deep Mahogany Stain Ritual

01. Fresh Sojat Paste Hand-rolled cones infused with pure eucalyptus & clove oils—zero PPD or synthetic dyes.
02. Book 48–72h Prior Natural lawsone dye transitions from bright tangerine to rich crimson-mahogany over 48 hours.
03. 6–8 Hour Oil Care Leave paste on for 6–8 hours and remove gently with natural oil before water contact.
